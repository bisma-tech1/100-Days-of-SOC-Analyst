# 🛡️ Day 01: SOC Fundamentals & Security Operations | 100 Days Challenge

## 🚀 Mission Statement
To bridge the gap between academic qualifications and industry-grade skills. I have embarked on this 100-day journey to master Security Operations (SOC) and prove that **technical obsession (Zid) and consistent skill-building** outweigh traditional degrees.

## 📚 Core Technical Competencies Acquired (Day 1)

### 1. Security Operations Center (SOC) Overview
Understood the role of a **SOC Analyst L1** as the first line of defense. Responsibilities include real-time monitoring of security alerts and performing initial analysis to distinguish between **True Positives** and **False Positives**.

### 2. Strategic Importance of SOC
Analyzing why organizations invest in SOC: 24/7 continuous monitoring to detect anomalies and mitigate threats before they escalate into full-scale breaches.

### 3. Understanding Dwell Time
Gained insights into the concept of **Dwell Time**—the duration a threat actor remains undetected within a network. 
*   **Objective:** Minimize the Mean Time to Detect (MTTD).

### 4. The Functional Landscape: SOC vs. NOC vs. IR
*   **NOC (Network Operations Center):** Focuses on availability and performance.
*   **SOC (Security Operations Center):** Focuses on security posture and detection.
*   **IR (Incident Response):** The tactical team responsible for neutralizing threats.

### 5. Alert Triage & Prioritization
Mastered the **Triage Process**: Systematic prioritization of incidents based on severity and asset criticality (The "Emergency Room" model).

### 6. Operational Challenges
Identified critical pitfalls such as **Alert Fatigue** and the importance of precise **Documentation/Reporting**.

### 7. The SOC Arsenal (Tooling)
*   **SIEM:** Centralized log management (e.g., Splunk).
*   **EDR/XDR:** Endpoint-level visibility (e.g., CrowdStrike).
*   **Threat Intelligence:** Global threat feeds (e.g., VirusTotal).

### 8. Threat Actor Profiling
Studied adversaries: Script Kiddies, Cyber Criminals, and Nation-State Actors (APTs).

### 9. Career Progression Framework
Mapped out the trajectory: L1 Analyst (Triage) ➔ L2 (Investigation) ➔ L3 (Threat Hunting) ➔ CISO.

### 10. Daily Operations & Shift Management
Understanding 24/7 operations, including shift handovers and incident lifecycle management.
